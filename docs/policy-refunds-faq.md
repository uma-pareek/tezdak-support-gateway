# Refund FAQ (customer-facing copy)

Source: help-centre page, copied into the repo on 2026-08-20 so the assistant text can be checked against it.

**How long do I have to ask for a refund?**
You can ask for a refund within 5 days of delivery.

**Does it matter if I paid online or by cash on delivery?**
No. The same 5 days apply to both.

**Will I get the money straight away?**
Online payments go back to your Tezdak wallet. Cash on delivery refunds are paid to a UPI id that you give us.
UPI payouts carry a small fee and cannot be reversed, so please check the id before you send it.

**Is there a limit?**
Refunds above Rs 2000 are approved by a person on our team. This can take up to one working day.

**What is goodwill credit?**
A small credit we may give when a delivery went wrong on our side. It is given once per customer.

**My parcel was marked delivered but I did not receive it.**
Tell us on chat. A team member will look at it with you.

**Who do I contact if I am not happy?**
Reply "agent" in the chat and a person will pick it up.
