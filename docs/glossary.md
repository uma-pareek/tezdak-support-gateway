# Glossary

- **AWB**: air waybill, the parcel's tracking number. Ours look like `TZ4829301`.
- **NDR**: non-delivery report. The rider's record that a delivery attempt did not succeed, with a reason.
- **RTO**: return to origin. The parcel goes back to the sender, normally after 3 attempts.
- **OTP**: one-time code the customer reads out to the rider at hand-over.
- **COD**: cash on delivery. The customer pays the rider; refunds go out by UPI.
- **Prepaid**: paid online. Refunds go to the Tezdak wallet.
- **Hub**: the local sorting and dispatch building a parcel passes through before the rider takes it.
- **Out-for-delivery (OFD)**: the parcel is on the rider's run today.
- **Attempt**: one rider run to a customer's door. A parcel gets 3.
- **Slot**: the day a new attempt is booked for: today, tomorrow or a date.
- **Cutoff**: 11:00 IST, the time same-day slots close.
- **Hub override**: a hub manager's approval for something beyond the usual rules, such as one more attempt.
- **Goodwill**: a small credit given when service fell short.
- **VPA**: UPI id of the form `name@bank`.
- **Tier**: `metro` or `tier2`, the kind of city the pincode belongs to.
