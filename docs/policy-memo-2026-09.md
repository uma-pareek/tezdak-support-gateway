# Memo: refund and goodwill rules from October

From: Support Operations and Finance. Dated 2026-09-29. Applies from 2026-10-15 unless the lead says otherwise.

## Refund window
- 10 days from delivery for prepaid orders.
- 7 days from delivery for COD orders.

## Human approval
Any refund or goodwill above Rs 3000 needs a person to approve it.

## Category limits
| Category | Limit (Rs) |
|---|---|
| electronics | 3000 |
| apparel | 1500 |
| grocery | 800 |
| other | 2000 |

## Goodwill
Goodwill is capped at Rs 100 per customer per rolling 30 days.

## Hub override
From attempt 3, the hub manager may approve a refund outside the window.

## Open item
Finance to confirm whether the hub override also lifts the limit.
